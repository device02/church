Our Story

The Church of Christ was founded on the principles of love, compassion, and service to others."

We are a Christ-centred church committed to spreading the Gospel, building strong relationships with God, and transforming lives through the power of His Word. We believe that the church is more than a place of worship; it is a family where people find faith, love, hope, encouragement, and a sense of belonging.

Our desire is to create an atmosphere where everyone can experience God's presence, grow spiritually, discover their purpose, and develop a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ.

We are committed to nurturing spiritual growth, strengthening families, raising future leaders, supporting our communities, and creating opportunities for people of all ages to serve God and fulfil their divine purpose.

You are welcome here. Come and worship with us, grow in faith, and experience the love of God.

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, make disciples of all nations, and raise a generation of believers who live according to God's Word and positively impact their communities.

Our Vision

To become a spiritually vibrant and impactful church, transforming lives through worship, prayer, sound biblical teaching, evangelism, and Christian fellowship.

What We Believe

We believe in God the Father, Jesus Christ His Son, and the Holy Spirit. We believe that the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the foundation for our faith and daily living. We believe in the power of prayer, the importance of genuine worship, salvation through Jesus Christ, and the call to demonstrate God's love to everyone.
